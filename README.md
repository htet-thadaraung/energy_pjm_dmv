# The role of solar DERs in the PJM Interconnection

** Research Question ** 
How can solar photovoltaics help address rising energy demand within the PJM interconnection in the DMV area? 
---
** Summary ** 
This research project will explore the role solar photovoltaics (PVs) can play in addressing energy usage and shortage issues within the PJM Interconnection in the DMV (DC, Maryland, Virigina) area. A hypothesis for the current research question at hand is listed below: 

* Solar PVs can provide better grid reliability and flexibility, therefore, are able to address rising electricity demand by providing backstopping support. Since solar PVs are widely popular and somewhat accessible, solar PVs can maintain (and/or) drive costs down to a reasonable level.
